# Data licence

This covers the **data pack** (`lix-serve.tar.gz`, unpacked to `data/serve/`) published
with each release, and anything you build from it. The code is under the MIT licence
([LICENSE](LICENSE)).

## The data pack is released under the ODbL

The pack is a database built from the open datasets listed in
[ATTRIBUTION.md](ATTRIBUTION.md). Some of it comes from **OpenStreetMap**: the points of
interest (pubs, cafés, shops, libraries, leisure) and the indicators built from them. That
makes the pack a Derivative Database of OpenStreetMap, which the Open Database Licence
requires to be shared under the same licence. So the pack as a whole is made available
under the [Open Database Licence 1.0 (ODbL)](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/).

## What reuse requires

- **Keep the notices.** Every attribution in [ATTRIBUTION.md](ATTRIBUTION.md), including
  "© OpenStreetMap contributors", must stay with the data and with maps, screenshots and
  other works made from it. The pack includes ATTRIBUTION.md for that reason.
- **Share alike.** If you publicly share a changed or extended version of the database,
  share it under the ODbL too, or offer the changes. A map or chart made from the data (a
  Produced Work) can use any licence, but must credit the sources.
- **No endorsement.** None of the data publishers endorse this project or its scores.

## Not advice

The scores combine published statistics with judgement calls about weights and
thresholds, explained in [docs/methodology.md](docs/methodology.md). They describe
neighbourhoods of 1,000–3,000 residents, not individual streets or homes, and each source
has its own date. Use them to explore, not as property, financial or legal advice.
